🔒 No history, no account. Novara does not store your searches or what you watch — no search history, no watch history, no cookies, no saved list, nothing is written to storage by this page. Local documents are searched privately in your browser. Voice search, when you tap the microphone, uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to turn your words into a search — the audio is handled by your browser, and Novara stores nothing from it. Photos and videos below now show many more results in-page from every free source that allows it (Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive, Openverse, Dailymotion, PeerTube), plus one-tap “see all” links to Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Yahoo, Yandex and more — because no free site can hold Google's whole index, the full “all results” pages live on those search engines, and this page links you straight to them for the same query. Note: when you play a video, that video site itself will see that play, just like visiting it directly; this page does not record it.
